How to get VIP SeDiv access

Follow these steps after purchase. When your license .txt file is returned, VIP SeDiv is activated automatically.

VIP SeDiv access required

New members and users who are not VIP SeDiv must send a License Request with their payment receipt photo. When the license email returns the .txt license file, VIP SeDiv is activated automatically. Then you can use Active License SeDiv and all VIP SeDiv areas.

Step 1 — Create an account (or sign in)

Go to https://forum.hdd-land.com and register a new account, or sign in if you already have one. Use a real email address so you can receive Message Center notifications.

Step 2 — Open License Request

Open Message Center. In the left menu choose License Request.

Direct link: https://forum.hdd-land.com/vbdlmanager/sediv_license_request.php

Step 3 — Upload your payment receipt

Choose your payment receipt photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF), then click Send VIP / license request. A Message Center ticket is created and the receipt is emailed to the license team with a tracking token (example: VBDL-REQ-…………).

Step 4 — Wait for the license .txt file

The license team replies with your license as a .txt file. The system imports that reply into your same ticket automatically. Status changes from waiting for license to VIP active.

Step 5 — VIP SeDiv is activated automatically

You do not need a separate VIP approval click for a normal successful request. When the .txt license is received, VIP SeDiv access is turned on. If menus do not update immediately, refresh the page or sign out and sign in again.

Step 6 — Use Active License SeDiv

Open active license sediv from Message Center:

https://forum.hdd-land.com/vbdlmanager/sediv_active_license.php

Upload your .lic file. When activation returns a .src file, it is posted into your VIP ticket for download.

Status meanings

StatusMeaning
waiting for licenseReceipt sent. Waiting for the license .txt reply.
license receivedLicense arrived; VIP grant may still be finishing (rare).
VIP activeDone. You can use Active License SeDiv and VIP areas.

Important tips

  • Keep your tracking token (VBDL-REQ-…). It links your email reply to your ticket.
  • Upload a clear payment receipt photo so processing is not delayed.
  • VIP SeDiv is required before you can use Active License SeDiv.
  • For help, open your License Request ticket in Message Center or contact HDD LAND support.
